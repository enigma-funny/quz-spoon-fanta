Question 1531 :
The Damodar Valley Project concerns two states

  1. Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh
  2. West Bengal and Bihar
  3. Maharashtra and Karnataka
  4. Haryana and Punjab
   West Bengal and Bihar

Question 1532 :
Mayurakshi Project is an undertaking of the Government of

  1. Maharashtra
  2. West Bengal
  3. Andhra Pradesh
  4. Uttar Pradesh
   West Bengal

Question 1533 :
In Thanjavur delta ________ crop is cultivated three times in a year.

  1. Wheat
  2. Rice
  3. Sugarcane
  4. Groundnut
   Rice

Question 1534 :
Hindustan Machine Tools has its factories at

  1. Bengaluru (Karnataka)
  2. Kalamassery (Kerala)
  3. Pinjore (Haryana)
  4. All the three places above
   All the three places above

Question 1535 :
Soil of Western Rajasthan have a high content or

  1. Aluminium
  2. Calcium
  3. Nitrogen
  4. Phosphorus
   Calcium

Question 1536 :
There is no Scheduled Caste population in

  1. Punjab
  2. Nagaland
  3. Gujarat
  4. Manipur
   Nagaland

Question 1537 :
Which of the following is an east flowing river of the Peninsula?

  1. Tapi
  2. Narmada
  3. Mahanadi
  4. None of the above
   Mahanadi

Question 1538 :
Kalbaisakhis or Norwesters originate in

  1. Chhota Nagpur Plateau
  2. Karnataka Coast
  3. Coromandal Coast
  4. Malwa Plateau
   Chhota Nagpur Plateau

Question 1539 :
Loktak Lake, a big lake of North East India, is located in

  1. Assam
  2. Arunachal Pradesh
  3. Manipur
  4. Tripura
   Manipur

Question 1540 :
India's indigenous pigs generally give birth to ______ young ones at a time.

  1. 10 to 12
  2. 4 to 6
  3. 2 to 4
   4 to 6

Question 1541 :
River is called 'The Sorrow of the Bengal'.

  1. Sone
  2. Damodar
  3. Hooghly
  4. Kosi
   Damodar

Question 1542 :
The thermal power plant of Bokaro is located in

  1. Bihar
  2. Chhattisgarh
  3. Jharkand
  4. Odisha
   Jharkand

Question 1543 :
The satellite construction centre in India is at

  1. Peenya
  2. Sriharikota
  3. Thumba
  4. Bengaluru
   Peenya

Question 1544 :
Which of the following is not a multi-purpose project involving more than one State in India?

  1. Nagarjunasagar
  2. Damodar Valley Corporation
  3. Bhakra Nangal
  4. Chambal
   Nagarjunasagar

Question 1545 :
The first Alumina plant in India is situated at

  1. Kiriburu (Jharkhand)
  2. Ratnagiri (Maharashtra)
  3. Sirpur (Andhra Pradesh)
  4. Korba (Madhya Pradesh)
   Korba (Madhya Pradesh)

Question 1546 :
The Nilgiris are part of the

  1. Eastern Ghats
  2. Western Ghats
  3. Vindhyachal
  4. Tamil Nadu Hills
   Western Ghats

Question 1547 :
Railway passenger coaches are manufactured at

  1. Varanasi
  2. Chittaranjan
  3. Perambur
  4. Kolkata
   Perambur

Question 1548 :
Which of the following states is the Sugar Bowl of India?

  1. Uttar Pradesh
  2. Himachal Pradesh
  3. Tamil Nadu
  4. Andhra Pradesh
   Uttar Pradesh

Question 1549 :
Which of the following is a natural harbour?

  1. Visakhapatnam
  2. Mumbai
  3. Chennai
  4. Kolkata
   Mumbai

Question 1550 :
Which is the croup of languages spoken by the largest number of people in India?

  1. lndo-Aryan
  2. Dravidian
  3. Austro-Asiatic
  4. Sino-Tibetan
   lndo-Aryan

Question 1551 :
Which of the following is not a major rubber producing states?

  1. Karnataka
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Odisha
   Odisha

Question 1552 :
The Abhors are

  1. an Adivasi tribe in Bastar
  2. a clan of dacoits
  3. a tribe of Mongoloid blood
  4. extinct now
   a tribe of Mongoloid blood

Question 1553 :
Oil refinery located near the of field is

  1. Nunamati
  2. Visakhapatnam
  3. Mathura
  4. Koyali
   Nunamati

Question 1554 :
The McMahan Line is a boundary between

  1. India and China
  2. India and Pakistan
  3. India and Bangladesh
  4. India and Nepal
   India and China

Question 1555 :
Why does a farmer plough his fields in summer?

  1. To prevent alkalinity of soil
  2. To prevent acidity of soil
  3. To prevent the surface from becoming hard
  4. To fertilize the soil
   To prevent the surface from becoming hard

Question 1556 :
Which of the following is not one of the traditional Indian seasons (ritus)?

  1. Vasant
  2. Grishma
  3. Margsheersha
  4. Sharad
   Margsheersha

Question 1557 :
________ State has the biggest area in India.

  1. Maharashtra
  2. Madhya Pradesh
  3. Rajasthan
  4. Uttar Pradesh
   Rajasthan

Question 1558 :
In India the problem of utilisation of agricultural wastes is in

  1. Conversion of cellulose into sugar
  2. Conversion of bran into molasses
  3. Conversion of starch into alcohol
  4. Conversion of sugar into carbohydrates
   Conversion of cellulose into sugar

Question 1559 :
At which place will you find maximum sunlight in December?

  1. Kanyakumari
  2. Pune
  3. Kolkata
  4. Leh
   Kanyakumari

Question 1560 :
Lac is secreted by

  1. trees like rubber
  2. certain kinds of birds
  3. some animals
  4. a certain type of insect which feeds on the saps of trees such as Palas, Ber and Kusum
   a certain type of insect which feeds on the saps of trees such as Palas, Ber and Kusum
