Question 181 :
The original home of the gypsies was

  1. Egypt
  2. Russia
  3. India
  4. Persia
   India

  1. Delhi        
  2. Mumbai      
  3. Aizawl      
  4. None of these

  2. Nelson

  1. Tamil Nadu
  2. Kerala

  4. Andhra Pradesh

Question 185 :
The majority or the coal reserves found in India are of

  1. Tertiary formation
  2. Gondwana formation
  3. Quaternary formation
  4. Jurassic formation
   Gondwana formation

Question 186 :
The heart of CBD’ is known for:

  1. Business Center      
  2. Transition      
  3. Down Town    
  4. Suburb

Question 187 :
Who proposed the rank size rule and attempted to express this relationship in mathematical terms?

  1. Linsky      
  2. G. K. Zipf      
  3. Mark Jefferson  
  4. F. Auerbach
  G. K. Zipf

Question 188 :
The most important oil producing area of the USA is

  1. the Applachian region
  2. the Texas-Louisiana belt
  3. the Ohio-Indiana belt
  4. the California-Colorado belt
   the Texas-Louisiana belt

Question 189 :
Mumbai receives more rainfall than Pune because: [CPO SI 2003]

  1. Mumbai is on the windward side
  2. Pune is at a-greater elevation
  3. Mumbai is a coastal city
   Mumbai is on the windward side

Question 190 :
There are two types of crusts – the continental and the oceanic. The continental crust is

  1. thicker below the plains than below mountains
  2. thicker below the mountains than below the plains
  3. almost same all over
  4. thicker under the oceans than under the seas
   thicker below the mountains than below the plains

Question 191 :
Full form of HDI:

  1. Human Direct Index      
  2. Housing Direct Investment                
  3. Human Development Index  
  4. Hindustan Development Income
  Human Development Index

Question 192 :
How many populations are there in Class V towns classified by Census of India

  1.  5, 000 – 9, 999    
  2. 10, 000 – 19, 999        
  3. 50, 000 – 99, 9999      
  4. More than 1000, 000
  5, 000 – 9, 999

Question 193 :
The ocean currents allow the oceans to_____ year after year.

  1. become warmer
  2. become colder
  3. maintain the same temperature
  4. increase salinity
   maintain the same temperature

Question 194 :
Which of the following natural regions is inhabited by the Eskimos of Canada and Alaska, Lappas of Finland and the Samoyeds and Yakuts of Siberia?

  1. High Mountain region
  2. Polar Tundra
  3. The Taiga region
  4. Cool (Mid-Latitude) West margin region
   Polar Tundra

Question 195 :
Which one of the following towns is not situated along the Grand Trunk Road?

  1. Allahabad
  2. Lucknow
  3. Agra
  4. Kanpur
   Lucknow

Question 196 :
The average per capita water consumption of Aizawl city is estimated at:

  2. 50 lpcd      
  3. 64 lpcd      
  4. 72 lpcd

Question 197 :
How many categories of towns are there in Census of India classification of towns?

  1. 5

  3. 7        
  4. 8

Question 198 :
With reference to India, which one of the following statements is not correct? [IAS 2002]

  1. About one-third of the area of the country records more than 750 millimetres of annual rainfall.
  2. The dominant source of irrigation in the country is wells
  3. Alluvial soil is the predominant type of soil in the northern plains of the country
  4. The mountain areas account for about thirty percent of the surface area of the country
   The mountain areas account for about thirty percent of the surface area of the country

Question 199 :
The great one horned Indian Rhino is round in

  1. Corbett National Park
  2. Kanha National Park
  3. Kaziranga Game Sanctuary
  4. Periyar Game Sanctuary
   Kaziranga Game Sanctuary

Question 200 :
How many Zones are there in Harris and Ullman’s Multiple-Nuclei Model of Urban Land Use?

  1. 3 Zone      
  2. 6 Zone      
  3. 9 Zone      
  4. 12 Zone

Question 201 :
The first oil well in the world was drilled at

  1. Assam
  2. Japan
  3. Pensylvania
  4. Former USSR

Question 202 :
The salinity or the sea is maximum at the

  1. Indian Ocean
  2. Red Sea
  3. Baltic Sea
  4. Atlantic Ocean
   Red Sea

Question 203 :
How many Megacity are there in India?

  1. 2            
  2. 3            
  3. 4            
  4. 5
  5

Question 204 :
Which one of the following pairs is not correctly matched? [CDS 1999]

  1. Flood-prone area: North Bihar Plain
  2. Drought-prone area: Kalahandi district of Orissa
  3. Seismic zone: Himalayan region
  4. Landslide zone: Chhotanaqpur plateau
   Landslide zone: Chhotanaqpur plateau

Question 205 :
What is the date of summer solstice in Northern Hemisphere?

  1. 21st June
  2. 21st March
  3. 22nd December
  4. 23rd September
   21st June

Question 206 :
Postmodern theory began to exert an influence on Urban Geography in:

  1. 1920 ‘s      
  2. 1940’s            
  3. 1960’s      
  4. 1980’s
  1980’s

Question 207 :
The total slum population in Greater Mumbai is around:

  1. 3 million        
  2. 6 million        
  3. 9 million        
  4. 12 million
  9 million

Question 208 :
The Buckingham Canal, which has been declared a National Waterway of India recently, is a canal running from

  1. Punjab to Haryana
  2. Mumbai to Goa
  3. Vijayawada to Villupuram
  4. Cochin to Kozhikode
   Vijayawada to Villupuram

Question 209 :
Wind erosion is dominant in

  1. Tropical regions
  2. Temperate regions
  3. Deserts
  4. Mediterranean regions

  3. 1, 2 and 3
