Question 2071 :
‘S = ei – et/ Et.Ei’ is a formula of _____________________________.

  The Index of Surplus Workers

Question 2072 :
 As per United Nation criteria, the minimum population which is needed for the area called as a city ______________________.

  20, 000

Question 2073 :
Which one of the following countries is planning to construct a rival to the Panama Canal to link the Pacific and Atlantic Oceans? [IAS 2007]

  1. Colombia
  2. Costa Rica
  3. Guatemala
  4. Nicaragua
   Nicaragua

Question 2074 :
Based on the functional classification of cities of USA, Chauncey D. Harris classified the towns into ____________ categories.

  9 (Nine)

Question 2075 :
Coalesce of Cities is term as _____________________.

  Conurbation

Question 2076 :
A census town must have a total population of at least ___________________.

  5, 000

Question 2077 :
Which is the largest fresh water lake in the world ? [CDS 2002]

  1. Lake Tanganyika
  2. Lake Superior
  3. Lake Baikal
  4. Lake Michigan
   Lake Superior

Question 2078 :
The earth rotates around its axis from: [RRB 1991]

  1. north to south
  2. east to west
  3. south to north
  4. west to east
   west to east

Question 2079 :
As per 2011 census, the slum households in the country are about ________________.

  13/14 million

Question 2080 :
MLD is stand for ________________________________________.

  Millions of Liters per Day

Question 2081 :
The word ‘urban’ is derived from __________________.

  Latin

Question 2082 :
The largest slum area in India is found in the city of ________________.

  Mumbai

Question 2083 :
A comprehensive theory of which explain the size spacing and interrelationship of settlement is called ______________________________.

  Central Place Theory

Question 2084 :
The largest fish exporting region in the world is:

  1. The South - East Asian Region
  2. The North - West Pacific Region
  3. The North - East Pacific Region
  4. The North - East Atlantic Region
   The North - West Pacific Region

Question 2085 :
The standard of living in developed countries is _________________ as compared to Developing or Less Developed Countries.

  High

Question 2086 :
Which one of the following countries has the highest average of road length on per thousand square kilometer area basis? [IAS 2004]

  1. India
  2. Japan
  3. USA
  4. France
   Japan

Question 2087 :
_______________ is the main reason for the growth of urbanization in developed countries.

  Industrial Revolution

Question 2088 :
Which of the following gives the correct decreasing order in numbers in the food chain pyramid? 1. Primary Producers 2. Herbivores 3. Carnivores 4. Man

  1. 1, 2, 3, 4
  2. 1, 3, 2, 4
  3. 4, 3, 2, 1
  4. 1, 2, 4, 3
   1, 2, 3, 4

Question 2089 :
The light coming from stars gives the idea of their:

  1. size
  2. rotational speed
  3. mass
  4. temperature
   temperature

Question 2090 :
The coastline formed by the submergence of mountain ridges running parallel to the coast is known as : [IAS 2004]

  1. Dalmation coast
  2. Ria coast
  3. Fiord coast
  4. Haff coast
   Dalmation coast

Question 2091 :
A ship met with an accident at 30ºC and 35ºN. The ship was sailing in the: [CDS 2000]

  1. Baltic Sea
  2. Black Sea
  3. Mediterranean Sea
  4. Red Sea
   Mediterranean Sea

Question 2092 :
The census of India defines an urban place with a minimum population of ______________

  5, 000

Question 2093 :
_______________ per cent of the world population live in the Urban Areas.

  30 per cent

Question 2094 :
Summer rains in Australia broadly decreases from [IAS 2009]

  1. east to west
  2. west to east
  3. north to south
  4. south to north
   north to south

Question 2095 :
A radio broadcast from Delhi on Monday at 7.30 p.m. is heard at New York on: [NDA 1994]

  1. Tuesday at 8.35 p.m.
  2. the same day at 7.30 p.m.
  3. the same day at 7:30 a.m.
  4. Sunday at 8.35 p.m.
   the same day at 7:30 a.m.

Question 2096 :
Which of the following is the longest Railway Tunnel?

  1. St. Gothard (Switzerland)
  2. East Finchley (London)
  3. Cascade (U.S.A.)
  4. Tanna (Japan)
   Tanna (Japan)

Question 2097 :
Of the following, which capital city is not situated on the banks of the river Danube?

  1. vienna
  2. Rome
  3. Budapest
  4. Belgrade
   Rome

Question 2098 :
Which of the following planets is known as 'Morning Star'?

  1. Mercury
  2. Venus
  3. Mars
  4. Jupiter
   Venus

Question 2099 :
There are _________ (number) zones in Hoyt’s Sector Model of Urban Land Use.

  5

Question 2100 :
Which one of the following is the correct sequence of the given towns of Pakistan while moving from the North towards the South ? [IAS 2005]

  1. Islamabad - Gujranwala – Peshawar - Multan
  2. Peshawar - Gujranwala - Multan - Islamabad
  3. Peshawar – Islamabad - Gujranwala - Multan
  4. Islamabad - Multan - Peshawar – Gujranwala
   Peshawar – Islamabad - Gujranwala - Multan
