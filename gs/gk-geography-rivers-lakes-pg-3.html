Question 61 :
Among the following areas, the widest continental shelf of India is found: [IAS 1991]

  1. around the Gulf of Cambay
  2. around the Konkan coast
  3. off the Krishna and Godavari deltas
  4. around the Palk strait
   off the Krishna and Godavari deltas

Question 62 :
Which one of the following gives the correct sequence of ports down the western coast of India?

  1. Kandla, Mumbai, Kochi, Mangalore
  2. Porbandar, Marmagao, Mangalore, Kochi
  3. Veraval, Okha, Marmagao, Mangalore
  4. Mangalore, Marmagao, Kochi, Negercoil
   Porbandar, Marmagao, Mangalore, Kochi

Question 63 :
Wular lake of Kashmir is a : [IAS 2003]

  1. Ox- bow lake
  2. Lake formed by blocking of ice
  3. Lake formed by terminal morainic dam

Question 69 :
Which of the following gives the correct descending order by length of the rivers of India?

Hinterland' refers to:
