Question 181 :
A narrow passage of water that connects two large bodies or water is known as

  1. Bay
  2. Isthmus
  3. Strait
  4. Archipelago
   Strait

Question 182 :
The essential feature of shifting cultivation is

  1. rotation of crops
  2. rotation of fields
  3. single cropping
  4. use of plenty of fertilizer
   rotation of fields

Question 183 :
______ is famous for its rubber production.

  1. Nigeria
  2. Ghana
  3. Malaysia
  4. Germany
   Malaysia

Question 184 :
Railways are best suited for

  1. passenger traffic for distances upto 1000 km
  2. freight traffic for bulky commodities
  3. urban commuters
  4. fast passenger movement
   freight traffic for bulky commodities

Question 185 :
Monsoon wind is different from planetary winds because it

  1. always brings rain
  2. blows in tropical regions
  3. crosses the Equator
  4. is seasonal
   is seasonal

Question 186 :
The land-locked country in South East Asia is

  1. Thailand
  2. Malayasia
  3. Laos
  4. Vietnam
   Laos

Question 187 :
The tropical deciduous trees shed their leaves in summer because

  1. of the heat of the summer
  2. the rainfall is inadequate
  3. it prevents loss of moisture through transpiration
  4. deciduous trees shed leaves
   it prevents loss of moisture through transpiration

Question 188 :
Condensation occurs when

  1. the air is cold
  2. the air is warm
  3. the air is saturated
  4. None of the above
   the air is cold

Question 189 :
A geographical mile is a measure of length equal to one sixtieth of a degree or one minute (1') of latitude. It varies with latitudes, but is approximately equals

  1. 7200 feet
  2. 6080 feet
  3. 4500 feet
  4. 6000 feet
   6080 feet

Question 190 :
The most effective farming method for returning minerals to the soil is

  1. Contour ploughing
  2. Terracing
  3. Crop rotation
  4. Furrowing

Question 191 :
Which of the following reflects radio waves back to the Earth?

  1. Ionosphere
  2. Thermosphere
  3. Tropopause

Question 192 :
The age of the Earth is approximately

  1. 1000 million years
  2. 4600 million years
  3. 3000 million years
  4. 100 million years

Question 193 :
Recycling' is a method for conserving the resources of

  1. coal
  2. oil
  3. copper
  4. limestone

Question 194 :
The capacity of the Atmosphere to hold water vapour

  1. increases as the temperature increases
  2. decreases as the temperature increases
  3. increases as the temperature decreases
  4. decreases as the temperature decreases
   increases as the temperature increases

Question 195 :
Which strait divides Europe from Africa?

  1. Bosporus
  2. Gibraltar
  3. Bering
  4. Dover
   Gibraltar

Question 196 :
Oases are examples of

  1. wet point settlements
  2. dry point settlements
  3. foothill settlements
  4. None of these
   foothill settlements

Question 197 :
Which or the following is the longest mountain range?

  1. The Himalayas
  2. The Rockies
  3. The Andes
  4. The Caucasus
   The Andes

Question 198 :
Which of the following absorbs the insolation from the sun and preserves the earth's radiated heat, thus acting like a blanket allowing the earth neither to become too cold nor too hot?

  1. Dust
  2. Water vapour
  3. Ozone
  4. Nitrogen
   Ozone

Question 199 :
River Rhine of Europe drains into

  1. North Sea
  2. Black Sea
  3. Caspian Sea
  4. Adriatic Sea
   North Sea

Question 200 :
The region with the lowest mean winter temperature (-50º C) is found in

  1. Greenland
  2. Northern Canada
  3. North Pole
  4. North Eastern Siberia
   Northern Canada

Question 201 :
Danube, Dneiper, Don, Volga and Ural rivers are flowing In

  1. North America
  2. Europe
  3. Africa
  4. Australia
   Europe

Question 202 :
Chemical weathering is a name given to

  1. disintegration of rocks without any change in their chemical composition
  2. chemical interaction of oxygen, carbon dioxide or water with the minerals in the rocks
  3. removal of soil by chemical action
  4. artificial breaking up of rocks for man's use
   chemical interaction of oxygen, carbon dioxide or water with the minerals in the rocks

Question 203 :
Which of the following yield teak?

  1. Coniferous forests
  2. Tropical forests
  3. Deciduous forests
  4. Himalayan forests
   Deciduous forests

Question 204 :
Which set of two riven given below together form the world's largest delta?

  1. The Ganges and the Brahmpautra
  2. The Mississippi and the Missouri
  3. The Tigris and the Euphrates
  4. The Seine and the Rhine

  1. 23rd September
