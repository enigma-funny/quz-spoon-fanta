Question 1861 :
Which of the following was not responsible for the spread of Buddhism?

  1. Truth, non-violence, compassion and toleration
  2. Truth, compassion, toleration and love
  3. Truth, love, sacrifice and generosity
  4. Love, compassion, elation at others success and equanimity towards all beings
   Love, compassion, elation at others success and equanimity towards all beings

Question 1862 :
The term ‘Yavan priya’ refers to—

  1. 1, 2, and 3
  2. 1,3and4
  3. 1, 2, and 4
  4. 2,3and4
   1,3and4

Question 1863 :
The first Maratha war took place during the Governor Generalship of—

  1. Warren Hestings
  2. Sir Robert Clive
  3. Lord Emherst
  4. Welleslley

Question 1873 :

  1. Ranjit Singh
  2. Mir Quasim
  3. Saadat Khan

Question 1875 :
The leaders of 1857 Mutineers who was caught - hanged was —

  1. Sindh
  2. Gujarat
  3. Punjab
  4. Both (a) and (b)
   Both (a) and (b)

Question 1876 :
Among other nations it is usual, in the contests of war to ravage the soil; among Indians, the tillers of soil, even when battle is raging in their neighbourhood, are undisturbed by any sense of danger and remain unmolesteel'—-This statement was made by —

  1. Huien Tsang
  2. Herodotus
  3. Megasthenese
  4. Fa Hien
   Huien Tsang

Question 1877 :
The Romans were famous for fights between man-man and man-animal which took place in

  1. Illiad
  2. Odefus rex
  3. Electra
  4. Odyssey
   Odefus rex

Question 1878 :
Which of the following is not true of both Buddhism - Jainism?

  1. Polity
  2. Economy
  3. Sociality
  4. None of these
   Economy

Question 1879 :
Gandhism is not merely a political creed it is

  1. A programme of action
  2. A message
  3. theory
  4. Working class movement
  A message

Question 1880 :
Official stamping of weights and measures and their periodical inspection are prescribed by —

  1. Manu
  2. Narada
  3. Brihaspati
  4. Parasara
   Manu

Question 1881 :
Homer, the great poet wrote

  1. Rocks
  2. Piece of stone
  3. Minerals
  4. Gold
   Rocks

Question 1882 :
The novelty which Kabir added to the Bhakti movement was —

  1. Qutubuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki
  2. Baba Farid-ud-din Chishti
  3. Shaikh Nizamuddin Chishti
  4. Nasiruddin ‘Chiragh’ Delhvi
   Shaikh Nizamuddin Chishti

Question 1883 :
Rigvedic people invoked Indra for—

  1. Only Brahmana girls
  2. Brahmana and Kshatriya girls
  3. Brahmana, Kshatriya and Vaisya girls
  4. Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaisya and Sudra girls
   Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaisya and Sudra girls

Question 1884 :
Given below are two statements. One labelled is Assertion (a) and the other labelled as Reason (R) — Assertion (a): Despite initial victory the Sepoy Mutiny could not succeed in overthrowing the Raj. Reason (R): The rising middle class sided with the Raj. In the context of the above two statements which one of the following is correct?

  1. Santhal Insurrection
  2. Kuki Rebellion
  3. Peasant agitation against the indigo planters in Bengal
  4. Agitation against reduction of age-limits for entry into Civil Service
   Peasant agitation against the indigo planters in Bengal

Question 1885 :
The Iqta under the Delhi Sultans was a territorial assignment and its holder was designated muqti who—

  1. The Sunga period
  2. The Kushana period
  3. The Gupta period
  4. The early medieval period
   The early medieval period

  3. Prof. Penka
