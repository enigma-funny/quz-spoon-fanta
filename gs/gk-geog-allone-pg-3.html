Question 61 :
Amritsar and Shimla have the same latitude yet Shimla is cooler than Amritsar because it:

  1. lies north of Amritsar
  2. comes under the influence of cold winds
  3. is at a higher elevation
  4. receives a lot of snow
   is at a higher elevation

Question 62 :
In which Zone slum area is generally located?

  1. CBD                  
  2. Commuters’ Zone              
  3. Zone in Transition              
  4. Residential Zone
  Zone in Transition

Question 63 :
Which one of the following rivers is not the tributary of Ganga ? [I. Tax - Central Excise 1993]

  1. Indrawati
  2. Son
  3. Gomati
  4. Yamuna
   Indrawati

Question 64 :
The climate of India is: [UP PCS 1994]

  1. tropical climate
  2. sub-tropical climate
  3. savanna type of climate
  4. subtropical monsoon
   subtropical monsoon

Question 65 :
The waterfall 'Victoria' is associated with the river

  1. Amazon
  2. Missouri
  3. St Lawrence
  4. Zambeji
   Zambeji

Question 66 :
Which one of the following shows the correct sequence of places in descending order of the average summer rainfall ? [CDS 1993]

  1. Jaisalmer, Pune, Allahabad, Cherapunji
  2. Cherapunji, Jaisalmer, Pune, Allahabad
  3. Allahabad, Pune, Jaisalmer, Cherapunji
  4. Cherapunji, Allahabad, Pune, Jaisalmer
   Cherapunji, Allahabad, Pune, Jaisalmer

Question 67 :
Which of the following rivers In Europe has the largest volume of traffic?

  1. The Rhine
  2. The Rhone
  3. The Danube
  4. The Seine
   The Rhine

Question 68 :
An area should be identified personally by the Charge Officer and also inspected by an officer nominated by the Director of Census operation. Such areas are considered as:

  1. Identified slums    
  2. Undeclared slums    
  3. Notified slums      
  4. Recognise slums
  Identified slums

Question 69 :
Which one of the following is not a site for 'in-situ' method of conservation of flora? [CSAT 2011]

  1. Biosphere Reserve
  2. Botanical Garden
  3. National Park
  4. Wildlife Sanctuary
   Botanical Garden

Question 70 :
Which is the smallest zone in Burgess Concentric Model?

  1. Zone I      
  2. Zone II          
  3. Zone III    
  4. Zone IV
  Zone I

Question 71 :
Which of the following is/are not the characteristics of red soils? 1. They are derived from weathering of old crystalline and metamorphic rocks 2. They contain iron oxides 3. They have high water retention capacity

  1. II and III
  2. Only III
  3. Only IV
  4. III and IV
   III and IV

Question 72 :
Podsol is a type of soil which is characteristic of a region under the cover of ____forest.

  1. deciduous
  2. coniferous
  3. evergreen
  4. alpine

Question 75 :
How many populations are there in Class I towns classified by Census of India

  1.  Less than 5, 000        
  2. 10, 000 – 19, 999        
  3. 50, 000 – 99, 9999

Question 76 :
As per 2011 census, the total population of the National Capital Territory or Metropolitan area of Delhi is around:

  2. 14 million      
  3. 16 million      
  4. 18 million

Question 77 :

  1. Cauvery Basin
  2. Godavari Basin
  3. Narmada Basin

Question 78 :

  1. Chennai
  2. Kolkata
  3. Shillong
  4. Thiruvananthapuram

Question 79 :

  2. Burgess  
  3. Christaller      
  4. None of these

Question 80 :

  1. 5, 000      
  2. 10, 000      
  3. 15, 000      
  4. 20, 000

Question 81 :
The soil formed by the deposition of silt brought by rivers is :

  1. alluvial soil
  2. red soil
  3. black soil
  4. pod soil
   alluvial soil

Question 82 :
Counter Urbanisation mostly happen in:

  1. Developed Countries          
  2. Developing Countries        
  3. Less Developed countries        
  4. None of these
  Developed Countries

Question 83 :
In developed countries, factors of production have been:

  1. Effectively Utilized    
  2. Ineffectively Utilized  
  3. No utilized          
  4. None of these
  Effectively Utilized

Question 84 :
 The ‘Multiple Nuclei Theory of Town Planning’ was given by:

  1. Burgess      
  2. Christaller      
  3. Ullman      
  4. Both ( and (
  Ullman

Question 85 :
The Himalayan region is poor in mineral resources because: [IAS 2004]

  1. it is made of crystalline rocks
  2. the displacement of rock strata has disturbed the arrangement of rock and made it complex
  3. the climatic conditions are not suitable for exploitation of minerals
  4. the terrain makes exploitation of minerals difficult and very costly due to transport difficulties
   the displacement of rock strata has disturbed the arrangement of rock and made it complex

Question 86 :
The formation of laterite soil is mainly due to:

  1. weathering
  2. erosion
  3. leaching
  4. deposition
   leaching

Question 87 :
How many towns are there in Mizoram?

  1. 8            
  2. 19          
  3. 23      
  4. 27

Question 88 :
For short-term climatic predictions, which one of the following events, detected in the last decade, is associated with occasional weak monsoon rains in the Indian subcontinent? [IAS 2002]

  1. La Nina
  2. Movement of jet streams
  3. El Nino and southern oscillations
  4. Green-house effect on global level
   El Nino and southern oscillations
