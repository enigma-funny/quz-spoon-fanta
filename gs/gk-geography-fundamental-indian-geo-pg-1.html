Question 1 :
Which of the following is NOT a food-grain?

  1. Wheat
  2. Rice
  3. Maize
  4. Cotton
   Cotton

Question 2 :
In which of the following states, Teesta Urja hydro-power project is proposed to be set up?

  1. Sikkim
  2. Andhra Pradesh
  3. West Bengal
  4. Arunachal Pradesh
   Sikkim

Question 3 :
Which of the following is not a part of the Great Plains?

  1. Indo-Gangetic Divide
  2. Ganga Delta
  3. Assam Valley
  4. Kerala Coastal Plain
   Kerala Coastal Plain

Question 4 :
Which State in India is known as the 'Granary of India'?

  1. Haryana
  2. Andhra Pradesh
  3. Punjab
  4. Kerala
   Punjab

Question 5 :
In India economically exploitable lignite deposits occur at

  1. Neyveli, Tamil Nadu
  2. Singareni, Andhra Pradesh
  3. Talcher, Odisha
  4. Jharia, Bihar
   Neyveli, Tamil Nadu

Question 6 :
The soils which have supported agriculture for centuries without much manuring or fallowing are

  1. alluvial and late rite soils
  2. red and laterite soils
  3. black and alluvial soils
  4. laterite and black soils
   black and alluvial soils

Question 7 :
The sun rises in Arunachal Pradesh two hours before it does in Dwaraka in Gujarat. This is because the former is

  1. higher in elevation than Dwaraka
  2. situated further north than Dwaraka
  3. situated further east (about 30º longitude) than Dwaraka
  4. situated about 30 º east of Dwaraka and the earth rotates from west to east
   situated about 30 º east of Dwaraka and the earth rotates from west to east

Question 8 :
Loktak is a

  1. Valley
  2. Lake
  3. River
  4. Mountain Range
   Lake

Question 9 :
The largest producer of wood pulp is

  1. India
  2. Canada
  3. Russia
  4. USA
   USA

Question 10 :
Arable lands refer to

  1. pasture land
  2. fallow land
  3. woodland
  4. cultivable land
   cultivable land

Question 11 :
The Islands of Lakshadweep are

  1. of volcanic origin
  2. of coral origin
  3. of continental origin
  4. elevated portions of sub-marine mountains
   of coral origin

Question 12 :
Main constituents of biogas are

  1. Methane and carbon monoxide
  2. Butane and carbon monoxide
  3. Methane and carbon dioxide
  4. Ethylene and carbon dioxide
   Methane and carbon dioxide

Question 13 :
Which city is served by Diamond Harbour?

  1. Mumbai
  2. Visakhapatnam
  3. Kolkata
  4. Rangoon
   Kolkata

Question 14 :
The Satpura range is situated between two west flowing rivers, They are

  1. Narmada and Luni
  2. Narmada and Tapi
  3. Tapi and Mahi
  4. Mahi and Luni
   Narmada and Tapi

Question 15 :
The wont affected state of India by the menace of sea erosion is

  1. Kerala
  2. Odisha
  3. Gujarat
  4. Andhra Pradesh
   Kerala

Question 16 :
Himadri is the term by which the _______ are known.

  1. Western Ghats
  2. Siwalik Ranges
  3. Middle Himalayas
  4. Greater Himalayas
   Greater Himalayas

Question 17 :
The Talcher Thermal Power Station is in

  1. Odisha
  2. Maharashtra
  3. West Bengal
  4. Meghalaya
   Odisha

Question 18 :
Belt of Doldrums exists in which of the following regions?

  1. Polar region
  2. Equatorial region
  3. Sub-Polar region
  4. Sub-Tropical region
   Equatorial region

Question 19 :
Tobacco was introduced into India by the

  1. Chinese
  2. Portuguese
  3. English
  4. French
   Portuguese

Question 20 :
The Himalayas are formed of parallel fold range a of which the oldest range is

  1. the Shiwalik Range
  2. the Lesser Himalayas
  3. the Great Himalayan Range
  4. the Dhaula Dhar Range
   the Great Himalayan Range

Question 21 :
Which is India’s largest multipurpose river valley project?

  1. Bhakra Nangal
  2. Damodar Valley
  3. Nagarjunasagar
  4. Thungabhadra
   Bhakra Nangal

Question 22 :
Anjuna, Baga, Colva, Majorda, and Miramar are beaches in

  1. Chennai
  2. Goa
  3. Mumbai
  4. Visakhapatnam
   Goa

Question 23 :
The Water Fall with the highest fall in India is the

  1. Jog Falls, Karnataka
  2. Courtallam Falls, Tamil Nadu
  3. Beraghat Falls, Madhya Pradesh
  4. Ettipotala Falls, Andhra Pradesh

Question 24 :
There is no Scheduled Tribe population in

  1. Kerala
  2. Tamil Nadu
  3. Punjab
  4. Rajasthan
   Punjab

Question 25 :

  1. intensive farming
  2. extensive farming

  4. plantation agriculture

Question 26 :

  1. Cotton
  2. Sugarcane

Question 27 :
The Ganga plain widens from

  1. south to north
  2. west to east
  3. north to south
  4. east to west
