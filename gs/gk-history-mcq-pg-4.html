Question 91 :
According to the estimate of W.H. Moreland the Population of India m A. D. 1600 was—

  1. Achyuta Patwardhan
  2. Jawaharlal Nehru
  3. Jayaprakash Narayana
  4. Acharya Narendra Dev
   Jawaharlal Nehru

Question 92 :
The Gupta gold and silver issues were initially based on the coins of the —

  1. Ochre coloured pottery
  2. Black and red ware
  3. Painted grey ware
  4. Northern black polished ware
   Painted grey ware

Question 95 :

  1. Never lived together
