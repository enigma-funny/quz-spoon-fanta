Question 541 :
India is particularly poor in reserves of

  1. coal
  2. iron ore
  3. non-ferrous metals
  4. limestone
   non-ferrous metals

Question 542 :
Which one of the following regions of India is now regarded as an 'ecological hot spot'?

  1. Western Himalayas
  2. Eastern Himalayas
  3. Western Ghats
  4. Eastern Ghats
   Western Ghats

Question 543 :
One of the states not bisected by the Tropic of Cancer is

  1. Odisha
  2. Gujarat
  3. West Bengal
  4. Rajasthan
   Odisha

Question 544 :
The Bhils and the Kols live

  1. along the Vindhya ranges
  2. in the Nilgiris
  3. in the North-East frontiers of India
  4. All over India
   along the Vindhya ranges
