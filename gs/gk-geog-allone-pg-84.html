Question 2491 :
Which of the following regions gets no rainfall throughout the year? [NDA 1992]

  1. The Equatorial Region
  2. The Tundra Region
  3. The Himalayan Region
  4. The Mediterranean Region
   The Tundra Region

Question 2492 :
Comets are celestial bodies moving about the solar system in

  1. elliptical or hyperbolic orbits, usually accompanied by a shining tail
  2. elliptical orbits usually accompanied by a long shining tail
  3. hyperbolic orbit always accompanied by a shining head
  4. hyperbolic orbit without projecting any tail or head
   elliptical orbits usually accompanied by a long shining tail

Question 2493 :
Which of the following is not helpful for delta formation? [CRPF 1990]

  1. Fast movement of river in mountains to cut sediments
  2. Slow speed
  3. Long river course
  4. Tidal nature of waves
   Tidal nature of waves

Question 2497 :

Question 2498 :
Consider the following statements : 1. Greenland is an overseas territory of France. 2. Berumuda, an overseas territory of UK, is situated in Western Atlantic. 3. State of Bahrain is situated in the Persian Gulf. Which of these statements is/are correct? [CPO AC 2003]

  1. 1, 2 and 3
  2. 2 only
  3. 2 and 3
  4. 1 and 3
   2 and 3

Question 2505 :
The position of the sun is annually twice overhead at Singapore because of the: [Railways 1993]

  1. rotation of the earth
  2. revolution of the earth
  3. elliptical path of the earth's orbit
  4. parallelism of inclined axis of the earth
   parallelism of inclined axis of the earth

Question 2508 :
Why are winters more severe in Southern Hemisphere than in the Northern Hemisphere?

  1. Earth is tilted towards the Sun in the Northern Hemisphere
  2. Northern Hemisphere receives more sunlight
  3. Because of more iceberg activity in Southern Hemisphere
  4. Southern Hemisphere is less inhabited
   Because of more iceberg activity in Southern Hemisphere

Question 2509 :

  1. Venus
  2. Mars
  3. Jupiter
  4. Saturn
   Venus

Question 2510 :

  1. Uruguay
  2. Guyana
  3. Surinam
  4. Ecuador
   Surinam

Question 2511 :
Which of the following is a reason for the increase in population of the developing countries?

  1. Increase in birth rate due to reluctance to accept family planning measures
  2. Increase in life expectancy and decrease of death rate due to medical facilities
  3. Both of these
  4. None of these
   Increase in life expectancy and decrease of death rate due to medical facilities

Question 2512 :
Which of the following is a low-density area? 1. Equatorial forest 2. Tropical deserts 3. Eastern Asia 4. North-eastern America

  1. I, II and IV
  2. I and II
  3. II and III
  4. I, III and IV
   I and II
