Question 1 :
The Great Barrier Reef is found

  1. close to Greenland
  2. near the Suez Canal
  3. in the Bay of Bengal
  4. of the coast of Queensland
   of the coast of Queensland

Question 2 :
Lava solidifies to form _____.

  1. Pumice
  2. Basalt
  3. Marble
  4. Sandstone
   Basalt

Question 3 :
____ is a land locked country.

  1. Bangladesh
  2. Myanmar
  3. Nepal
  4. Sri Lanka
   Nepal

Question 4 :
If A and B are two stations antipode to each other then their

  1. seasons are opposite
  2. day and night are opposite
  3. seasons day and night are opposite
  4. climates are opposite
   seasons day and night are opposite

Question 5 :
The yield or food grain per man is high under

  1. intensive cultivation
  2. extensive cultivation
  3. mono cultivation
  4. farm cultivation
   extensive cultivation

Question 6 :
Which European country has over 200 volcanoes, many of them active?

  1. Norway
  2. Finland
  3. Ireland
  4. Iceland
   Iceland

Question 7 :
The world's major fishing grounds are located along the eastern shores of continents between 40º N and 50º N latitudes where cool and warm currents converge. An example or such area is

  1. The Grand Banks off Newfoundland
  2. The Dogger Bank in the North Sea
  3. The coastal region of Norway
  4. Fisheries off the coast of Peru
   The Grand Banks off Newfoundland

Question 8 :
Which of the following is not one of the by-products of mineral oil?

  1. Naphtha
  2. Sulphur
  3. Aniline dyes
  4. Paraffin wax
   Sulphur

Question 9 :
If I wanted to reach the South Atlantic from the South Pacific while touching South America, I would have to use

  1. Florida Straits
  2. Dardanelles
  3. Magellan Straits
  4. Bosporus
   Magellan Straits

Question 10 :
The Trans-Siberian railway links

  1. The Baltic coast with the North Sea
  2. Leningrad with Oldiotsk
  3. Buanos Aires with Valparaiso
  4. Leningrad with Vladivostok
   Leningrad with Vladivostok

Question 11 :
A huge muss of ice fed by snow on a high mountain slowly sliding downhill before It starts melting is called

  1. Glacier
  2. Avalanche
  3. Iceberg
  4. Snow cover
   Glacier

Question 12 :
Canals serve as passage of communication in the city of ___in Italy.

  1. Rome
  2. Venice
  3. Turin
  4. Genoa
   Venice

Question 13 :
Barkhans are

  1. depositional features of wind action
  2. erosional features associated with rivers
  3. erosional features associated with wind action
  4. depositional features of glacial action
   depositional features of wind action

Question 14 :
Limestone is an example of

  1. igneous rocks
  2. sedimentary rocks
  3. metamorphic rocks
  4. crystalline rocks
   sedimentary rocks

Question 15 :
Which one of the following types of plains is not formed by the action of the riven?

  1. Loess plains
  2. Alluvial plains
  3. Flood plain deposits
  4. Deltas
   Loess plains

Question 16 :
Contours are lines showing

  1. places on earth of equal rainfall
  2. places of same temperature
  3. places on earth at the same altitude
  4. None of these
   places on earth at the same altitude

Question 17 :
Liverpool Hull, Bristol and Southampton are ports in

  1. America
  2. Europe
  3. Africa
  4. England
   England

Question 18 :
Mediterranean climate is ideal for growing

  1. wheat and barley
  2. tea and coffee
  3. sugarcane and beet
  4. orange and lemons
   orange and lemons

Question 19 :
_____ canal lies on one of the world's largest of trade routes connecting Europe and Far East.

  1. The Panama
  2. The Suez
  3. The Kiel
  4. The Goth
   The Suez

Question 20 :
The tropical deciduous trees shed their leaves in summer because

  1. of the heat of the summer
  2. the rainfall is inadequate
  3. it prevents loss of moisture through transpiration
  4. deciduous trees shed leaves
   it prevents loss of moisture through transpiration

Question 21 :
The world's largest river is

  1. Amazon
  2. Nile
  3. Mississippi
  4. Brahmaputra
   Amazon

Question 22 :
Which of the following layers of the Earth is believed to account for the Earth's magnetism?

  1. Crust
  2. Mantle
  3. Core
  4. Lithosphere
   Core

Question 23 :
Ozone layers absorb

  1. infrared rays
  2. ultraviolet rays coming from the sun
  3. all radiations from the sun
  4. cosmic rays
   ultraviolet rays coming from the sun

Question 24 :
Mediterranean climate prevails in

  1. Central Chile
  2. Northern Chile
  3. Peru
  4. Argentina
   Central Chile

Question 25 :
The Tropical Rain Forest or Equatorial type of climate is found in

  1. Amazon basin
  2. Congo basin
  3. Indonesian Islands
  4. All the above
   All the above

Question 26 :
By what name the Third World Countries are now commonly referred to?

  1. Least developed countries
  2. Underdeveloped countries
  3. Developing countries
  4. Underdeveloped countries

Question 27 :
Which of the following is taken into consideration while deciding a shipping route?

  1. Depth of the sea
  2. Availability of ports and harbours
  3. Climate of the region
  4. All the above

  3. control the level of water
