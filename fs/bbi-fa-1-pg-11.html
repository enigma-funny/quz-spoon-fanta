Question 301 :
In _____ method, stock is over valued when the prices are rising.

  1. FIFO
  2. Weighted Average
  3. LIFO
  4. All of the above
  FIFO

Question 302 :
Spent on making an entrance for a cinema house 1,50,000

  1. Revenue expenditure
  2. Capital expenditure
  3. Deferred revenue expenditure
  4. None of the above
  Capital expenditure

Question 303 :
Which concept provides a link between present and future?

  1. Going Concern
  2. Cost Concept
  3. Entity
  4. None of the above
  Going Concern

Question 304 :
Purchase of tools for servicing.

  2. Revenue
  3. D.R. Expenses
  4. None of the above

  2. expenses
  3. fictitious
  4. All of the above

Totalling of journal or ledger.

  4. None of the above

  1. profit
  2. loss

  4. All of the above

Question 308 :

  3. profit
  4. None of the above

Replacement of old tyres by new one

  4. None of the above

  4. None of the above

Question 311 :
Excess of H.P. price over cash price is _____.

  1. Interest

  3. Possession
  4. None of the above

Question 312 :
Goodwill is not a _____ asset.

  1. Realisable
  2. Intangible
  3. Fictious
  4. None of the above

As per _____ convention the organisation should follow the same method year after year.

  1. Disclosure concept

  4. None of the above

Question 314 :
Which system of Book-Keeping under which records of both the aspects of transactions are recorded?

  1. Double Entry System
  2. Indian System
  3. Deshi System

Question 315 :

  1. credit memo

  3. cash receipt

Question 316 :
The total capital of a company is divided into small parts of same value is called _____.

  4. All of the above

Question 317 :
AS 28 deals with _____ _____ _____.

  1. Presentation
  2. Earning per share
  3. Impairment of assets

Question 318 :
Initial amount paid on signing the agreement is called _____ _____.

  1. Possession
  2. Interest
  3. Down Payment
  4. None of the above
  Down Payment

Question 319 :
Super profit is

  1. Excess of future maintainable profit over normal profit.
  2. Average profit.
  3. Normal profit.
  4. Abnormal profit.
  Excess of future maintainable profit over normal profit.

Question 320 :
In the case of an overdraft, the bank column of the Cash Book will show a _____ balance.

  1. Credit
  2. Debit
  3. Bank
  4. None of the above

Question 321 :
_____ is a fund established for specific purpose.

  1. Revenue

  1. Revenue
